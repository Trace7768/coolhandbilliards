Hall of Hustler

Score 23 or more points in a single NAPA match and you're in. Each inductee gets a certificate from NAPA HQ and a permanent spot in NAPA's national Hall of Hustlers.

How do you get to 23?

Points for one match build like this:

  1. Show up for your match1
  2. Win a game3
  3. Reach the hill (one win from the match)7
  4. Win the match14
  5. Sweep: win without your opponent winning a game20

Getting to 23 takes a sweep plus at least 3 bonus points, worth +1 each time:

  • Snap — win a game on the break
  • Run out — clear every ball in a single turn at the table
  • Lag and sweep — win the lag, let your opponent pick the game format, then sweep them

23 is hard to get.
